# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Managers

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers lead the technical execution of a team by balancing delivery goals, team capacity, and engineering quality. They translate strategy into sustainable team operations and help remove delivery blockers.

### Responsibilities
- Plan team capacity and prioritize technical work
- Support sprint execution, dependency tracking, and milestone delivery
- Coach engineers and improve engineering practices
- Align technical decisions with product and project goals
- Monitor team health, risk, and operational readiness

### Goals
- Improve delivery predictability and team effectiveness
- Maintain a healthy engineering operating rhythm
- Reduce friction between product priorities and engineering execution

### Typical Communication
- Weekly engineering and project syncs
- Capacity and staffing conversations with PM and Product leads
- Technical risk updates and dependency escalations

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Project Managers on schedules, risk, and cross-team coordination
- Collaborates with Developers on technical planning, technical debt, and implementation quality
- Aligns with Product Managers to ensure roadmap priorities are realistic and actionable

---

## QA / Quality Leads

### Role Summary
QA / Quality Leads define and govern the quality bar for releases and features. They ensure that what is delivered meets business expectations, acceptance criteria, and operational standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, validation plans, and release readiness criteria
- Triage defects and coordinate issue resolution across teams
- Validate acceptance criteria and user-facing quality expectations
- Support quality gates for planning, execution, and release
- Identify recurring quality risks and recommend improvements

### Goals
- Prevent defects from reaching production unnecessarily
- Increase confidence in releases and feature quality
- Ensure releases meet both product and operational expectations

### Typical Communication
- QA signoff reviews and defect triage sessions
- Release readiness check-ins with PM, Product, and Engineering leads
- Acceptance validation with stakeholders and support teams

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers to ensure acceptance criteria are measurable and testable
- Works with Developers to validate fixes and improve test coverage
- Coordinates with Project Managers on release timing, risk review, and readiness status

---

## Security / Compliance Partners

### Role Summary
Security / Compliance Partners safeguard the organization from operational, data, and product risks by reviewing architecture, process, and implementation choices against security and policy requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Review systems, workflows, and changes for security and compliance risks
- Define or enforce security requirements, access controls, and governance expectations
- Support risk assessments and incident response readiness
- Ensure projects meet required policies and approval checkpoints
- Escalate high-risk concerns and mitigation plans early

### Goals
- Reduce risk exposure and protect customer trust
- Support secure delivery without slowing critical work unnecessarily
- Build confidence in governance and compliance practices

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and architectural discussions
- Risk and compliance checkpoints during planning and release
- Incident escalation and mitigation updates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Advises Developers and Engineering Managers on secure design and implementation practices
- Coordinates with Project Managers to account for security review timelines and release dependencies
- Works with Product Managers and stakeholders to balance business goals with security and compliance needs

---

## Data / Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data / Analytics Leads ensure teams can define, measure, and evaluate success using clear metrics and evidence. They turn business goals into measurable indicators and reporting frameworks.

### Responsibilities
- Define KPIs, success metrics, and instrumentation needs
- Partner on experimentation, reporting, and data quality checks
- Provide insight into user behavior, business impact, and performance trends
- Support decision-making with objective evidence and dashboards
- Connect product outcomes to operational and business value

### Goals
- Make impact measurable and understandable
- Improve evidence-based decision making
- Ensure metrics support product learning and accountability

### Typical Communication
- Product and roadmap metric reviews
- Dashboard and reporting updates for stakeholders
- Performance and outcome discussions during planning and release review

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to define success criteria and measurement plans
- Supports Project Managers with milestone and impact tracking
- Provides Developers and Engineering Managers with the data needed to validate product outcomes and operational health

---

## Customer Success / Support Liaisons

### Role Summary
Customer Success / Support Liaisons connect the delivery team to customer needs, operational realities, and service quality outcomes. They bring direct feedback into prioritization and delivery decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Capture customer feedback, support trends, and adoption issues
- Escalate customer-impacting blockers and known pain points
- Support readiness for launch, onboarding, and service continuity
- Help translate customer problems into product and process improvements
- Coordinate with stakeholders on communication and service expectations

### Goals
- Improve customer experience and retention
- Reduce friction between internal teams and external stakeholders
- Ensure customer needs influence planning and execution

### Typical Communication
- Customer readiness and support briefings
- Feedback loops with Product and PM teams
- Incident and post-release follow-up communication

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Shares user and service insights with Product Managers and delivery teams
- Advises Project Managers on rollout readiness and stakeholder communication needs
- Supports Developers and QA teams with real-world usage scenarios and issue patterns

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Together, these roles clarify accountability, handoffs, and decision ownership across planning, execution, release, and stakeholder communication.
